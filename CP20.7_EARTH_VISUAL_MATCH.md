# CP20.7 — HOME EARTH VISUAL MATCH

This patch addresses the remaining visual gap visible after CP20.6.

## What changed

The supplied target is a polished three-dimensional Earth command center:
Europe/Africa are visible in the middle, Americas sit to the left, Asia to the
right, with a larger Earth, stronger atmosphere, orbital rings, a central N
core, and a smaller integrated platform.

CP20.7 changes only:

- `components/home/HolographicEarth.tsx`
- `components/home/HolographicEarth.module.css`

Do not modify `HomeCommandCenter.tsx`, Landing, or Supabase.

## Main corrections

1. Earth starts at longitude 0 instead of the previous 180-degree offset.
2. Earth is larger and closer to the camera, especially on mobile.
3. Platform is scaled down and moved under the sphere.
4. Adds the glowing N core directly over the Earth.
5. Adds more orbital rings and route arcs.
6. Moves region HUD panels outside the globe instead of stacking over it.
7. Uses stronger atmospheric rim and brighter land/coast treatment.
8. Keeps the frozen Landing Earth texture.
9. Keeps the CP20.6 safe renderer initialization.
10. Keeps mobile DPR/geometry reduction and reduced-motion support.
