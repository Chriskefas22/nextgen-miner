# CP20.9 — SYNCHRONIZED EARTH COMMAND CENTER

## Why CP20.8 still looked wrong

The screenshot showed:
- route/city lines crossing the globe,
- multiple circles visually drifting or cutting through the Earth,
- the central N core missing/obscured,
- a very heavy grid,
- HUD labels colliding with the globe.

The main architectural issue was not color; it was the scene hierarchy.

## CP20.9 architecture

ONE master transform:

commandRoot
  └─ synchronizedSystem
       ├─ Earth
       ├─ subtle globe lattice
       ├─ atmosphere
       ├─ surface network dots
       └─ orbital rings

There is NO city-to-city route system.

The pedestal is separate and static under the globe.
Its two glow rings are not independent orbit systems.

## Replace exactly

- `components/home/HolographicEarth.tsx`
- `components/home/HolographicEarth.module.css`

Do not replace `HomeCommandCenter.tsx`.

## Visual changes

- Reuses the frozen Landing Earth texture.
- Europe/Africa are the intended central orientation.
- Americas appear left; Asia appears right.
- Removes all city/network connection lines.
- Keeps only subtle surface network dots.
- Puts all Earth + rings + surface nodes under one synchronized master rotation.
- Reduces globe grid to a near-invisible detail layer.
- Adds a clearly visible central N Core.
- Adds three thin orbital rings with controlled depth.
- Adds a low-profile pedestal.
- Keeps live/paused, asset, hashrate, miners, and output HUD driven by Home props.
- Desktop pointer parallax remains.
- Mobile renderer uses lower DPR/geometry.
- Reduced motion supported.
- WebGL init/render failures are isolated from the dashboard.

## Data boundary

The renderer performs no Supabase query and no economic mutation.

All values come from the existing Home snapshot:
`asset`, `status`, `activeHashrate`, `activeMiners`, `dailyOutputUsd`.

## Frozen areas

Do not change:
- `components/landing/NetworkCore.tsx`
- `components/landing/LandingPage.tsx`
- Supabase SQL/RPC
- settlement
- recharge
- daily check-in
- economic capacity guard
