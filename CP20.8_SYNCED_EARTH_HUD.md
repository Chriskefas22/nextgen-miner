# CP20.8 — SYNCHRONIZED EARTH HUD

## Purpose

Fix the remaining visual synchronization problem in Home.

The reported screenshot showed the Earth rotating independently from the
decorative circles and fixed HUD labels, which made the whole visual feel
misaligned.

## Core fix

All 3D Earth visual elements now share one master `earthSystem`:

- Earth
- globe grid
- atmosphere
- violet atmosphere
- regional nodes
- orbital rings
- pedestal/platform

The Earth rotation is applied only to `earthSystem`, so those 3D elements
remain synchronized.

Region HUD labels are no longer hard-positioned. Their screen position is
projected from the same 3D node coordinates every frame. Labels disappear
when the associated node is on the far side of the Earth.

## City/network lines

Surface region-to-region connection lines have been intentionally removed.

Nodes remain as points only, with no "city lines" crossing the globe.

## Visual source

The Earth texture remains the frozen Landing asset:

`/assets/landing/earth-equirectangular.webp`

## Data boundary

Home passes only presentation metrics to the renderer:

- asset
- status
- activeHashrate
- activeMiners
- dailyOutputUsd

No Supabase queries or economic mutations are performed here.

## Files to replace

- `components/home/HolographicEarth.tsx`
- `components/home/HolographicEarth.module.css`

No changes to `HomeCommandCenter.tsx` are required.

Do not modify:

- `components/landing/NetworkCore.tsx`
- `components/landing/LandingPage.tsx`
- Supabase SQL/RPC
- economic engine

## Expected visual behavior

1. Earth rotates slowly.
2. Rings rotate with the same Earth system and never drift away.
3. Nodes stay attached to their Earth coordinates.
4. Region labels follow their nodes and hide behind the globe.
5. No region-to-region/city connection lines are visible.
6. Pedestal remains synchronized under the globe.
7. HUD metrics remain fixed and data-driven by Home snapshot.
8. Mobile remains lighter than desktop.
9. Reduced motion still disables continuous animation.

## Manual deployment

`/dashboard`.

Retest on Android first.

Look specifically for:

- no diagonal/city lines crossing Earth
- no orbit drift

- all three rings moving coherently
