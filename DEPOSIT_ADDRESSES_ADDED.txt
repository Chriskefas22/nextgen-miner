NextGen Miner — owner-controlled manual deposit destinations.

Configured in Supabase:
BNB, LTC, BTC, BCH, ETH, DOGE, DASH, ADA, DGB,
USDT TRC20, USDT ERC20, USDT BEP20, USDC (Solana/SPL),
TRX, TON, XLM, SOL, XRP, POL, PEPE (ERC20), XMR.

All supplied addresses are stored as Manual destinations and active.
QR codes are bundled under assets/deposit/qr/.

BNB retains the user-specified metadata: minimum deposit 0.000001 BNB,
6 confirmations, network fee 0.

For the other destinations, coin-specific minimum deposit, confirmations,
and network fee were not supplied, so those fields remain NULL until the
owner explicitly configures them. The website-wide funding limits remain
controlled by the economy/funding settings.

Network assumptions used where the user supplied an address without a network:
USDC address -> Solana/SPL, POL address -> Polygon PoS, PEPE address -> Ethereum ERC20.
These should be verified by the owner before accepting live deposits.
