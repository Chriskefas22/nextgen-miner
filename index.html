SUPPORT

Frequently Asked.

How are hourly, daily, and monthly figures calculated?

They are derived from the plan-level configuration stored in Supabase. The dashboard uses the hourly value and server-side elapsed time to accrue earnings.

Can I use all 50 levels?

Yes. Each of the five rig families has levels 1–50. The interface lets you inspect and activate a specific level once your account has enough available balance.

Is withdrawal automatic?

No. Withdrawal requests are created as pending records and can be reviewed/approved before being processed.
